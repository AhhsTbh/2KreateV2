Tips: VC and MyPOINTS

From 2K's own pages and community guides, checked Oct 3, 2026. 2K hasn't published exact earn rates, so numbers below are reports, not guarantees.

MyPOINTS

These raise your max overall, so they are what takes a build toward 99.

  • Finish the MyCAREER NBA intro (player tip). Players say finishing the opening NBA stretch of MyCAREER unlocks a 20% MyPOINTS boost, so do it early.
  • Street Kings (player tip). One player reports about 16,000 MyPOINTS in a good Street Kings game, which is well above the usual range.
  • Play to earn them. The in-game upgrade screen says MyPOINTS are how you raise your max overall cap. The MyPOINTS Accelerator reward is earned in The City.
  • Stack the stat sheet. Guides report roughly 7,000 to 12,000 MyPOINTS from a strong game. Steals, blocks and rebounds count, so don't only score.
  • Short games farm best. Guides suggest ending games fast with big stat lines, and only playing longer when you're chasing badge progress.
  • Use boosts first. Turn on any MyPOINTS or XP boost before you start a session so every game counts more.
  • Stack goals. Pick modes where MyPOINTS, Season XP, badge progress and Build Specialization all move in the same game.

VC

  • Open Court (player tip). Players recommend Open Court as a good way to earn VC.
  • You can earn it free. VC comes from playing MyCAREER, The City, Seasons and daily rewards. Buying it is optional, and 2K hasn't published per-game rates.
  • Log in daily. The NBA 2K HQ app has its own daily login rewards that carry over to your console.
  • Run the events. Limited-time events like The Deep End pay VC and boosts. Check the Events list in the Build Lab tab.
  • Level the Season ladder. Season 1's 40 levels also give Skill Boosts, Cap Breakers, Badge Tokens and Badge Slots, so leveling pays more than VC.
  • Redeem locker codes fast. Codes that give VC tend to expire within days.

Spend it smart

  • Plan first. Upgrades are permanent. Build it here or in the in-game builder before you spend.
  • Stop at the badge threshold. Use the "Next" hint on each badge and don't pay for points that unlock nothing.
  • You don't need to max everything. Reaching 99 overall takes a budget, not every attribute at its cap. Lower what doesn't unlock a badge.
  • Cap Breakers are limited. Five per attribute at most, permanent, and they can't go past your body's cap. Low attributes get bigger boosts. Build Specialization ones only go in your category or Physicals.
  • Cap Breaker boosts scale with your build. In CVP's builder preview, an attribute at 25 gained only +3 per breaker on an empty build but +13 once the rest of the build was filled in, and low attributes gained far more than high ones. 2K only shows the exact boosts at 99 overall, so check there before you spend.
  • Sometimes normal upgrades win. Regular attribute points can give more badge value than a Cap Breaker, so compare first.

How timing works

From community shooting tests: green here is the pure window, where a release always counts, about 15 to 20 ms wide on a typical jumper. The window drifts a millisecond or two from shot to shot, so aim for the middle and not the edge. The make rate above already includes that drift. Stamina, adrenaline, the catch, contests and distance all slide the window earlier or later, and each release speed notch moves it about 15 ms. Ratings don't make the pure window bigger. They widen the forgiving shoulder around it.
